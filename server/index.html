It’s in the server’s log (docker compose logs fluidmessages, or the container’s Logs in Portainer). Or open FluidMessages on the server’s own computer, where no code is needed.

At least 8 characters. A few words together is easy to remember and hard to guess.

Lost your phone? Type one of your recovery codes instead, like XXXX-XXXX-XXXX.

Forgot your password?

Open FluidMessages on the computer it runs on: it opens there without a password, and Settings → Phone and other devices sets a new one. Or, on the server, run docker compose exec fluidmessages python main.py reset-password. Your messages and accounts stay.

Lost your authenticator app and your recovery codes?

Open FluidMessages on the computer it runs on and turn two-step sign-in off in Settings → Phone and other devices. No code is needed there. Or, on the server, run docker compose exec fluidmessages python main.py reset-two-step. Then sign in with just your password.
