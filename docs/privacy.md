# Privacy policy

*Written in plain words. Last updated with version 0.41.0.*

## The short version

FluidMessages has **no ads, no tracking, no analytics, and no account with us**. We don't run any server that your messages go through. There is nothing for us to collect, so we don't collect anything.

## Where your messages go

- **Texts and picture messages** go through your phone company, like any texting app.
- **Chat services** (Matrix, XMPP, Email, IRC) go to the servers *you* chose when you added the account.
- **FluidMessages Web** runs on a computer you control. If you link your phone, your texts are copied to that computer.
- **Backups** are locked with your password and saved only where you choose.

## When the app contacts other websites

The app only reaches out to other sites when a feature needs it:

| What | Which site | When |
|---|---|---|
| Update check | GitHub | About once a day, if you installed from GitHub. Turn it off in **Settings → About and updates**. |
| GIFs | The GIF sites you turned on | Only when you search for GIFs. |
| Link previews | The website in the link | Automatically for your own messages and saved contacts. For anyone else, only when you tap **Tap to load preview**. |
| Meeting links | Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, or Jitsi | Only when you start or join a meeting. Those companies can see who joins. |
| Account setup help | Your email provider's help page | Only when you tap the button. |

## What stays on your phone

Your contacts, messages, drafts, themes, and settings stay on your devices. Notification text is shown only on your phone, and is hidden for the private folder, while app lock is on, and on a secure lock screen.
